# Data licence

The Atlas's own data and writing are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
That covers everything under `data/` and the chapter and methodology text.

You may share and adapt them for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you credit
**The Software Atlas** and link to the licence.

**Third-party figures are not ours to license.** Each data point cites the publisher it comes from
(Gartner, IDC, OECD, US Census Bureau, company filings and others). CC BY 4.0 covers our selection,
arrangement and annotation of those figures, and the short quotes kept for verification. It does not
cover the underlying reports. Check the original publisher's terms before reusing a figure on its own.

The code is licensed separately under the MIT licence (see `LICENSE`).
