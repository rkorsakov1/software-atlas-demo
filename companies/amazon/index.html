Events (10)

  1. March 2006Platform shift

    Amazon launches S3, followed by EC2, and creates cloud infrastructure

    New-market disruption in its purest form: the first customers were startups that enterprise IT did not serve, and utility pricing expanded demand faster than it cut margins.

    AmazonCloud infrastructure servicesS62, S63
  2. January 2021Licence change

    Elastic relicenses Elasticsearch and Kibana under SSPL and the Elastic License

    The opening move of the commercial open-source licence wars: a defence against hyperscaler resale that cost the project its open-source status.

    ElasticAmazonDocument, key-value and vector-capable NoSQLObservability and monitoringS54
  3. April 2021Spin-off

    AWS forks Elasticsearch as OpenSearch

    Demonstrated the ceiling on restrictive relicensing: a hyperscaler with engineering capacity can fork from the last open commit and keep the community.

    AmazonElasticDocument, key-value and vector-capable NoSQLS57
  4. September 2023Regulation

    The European Commission designates six gatekeepers under the Digital Markets Act

    Moved platform regulation from case-by-case litigation to ex-ante obligations, the biggest structural change in software competition law since US v. Microsoft.

    GoogleAmazonAppleByteDanceMetaMicrosoftMobile operating systems and app distributionSocial and messagingMarketing and advertising technologyS67
  5. March 2024Unbundling

    Digital Markets Act obligations take effect for designated gatekeepers

    Alternative app stores, third-party payment options and choice screens arrived in the EU, creating the first large natural experiment in whether mandated unbundling shifts share.

    AppleGoogleMetaAmazonMicrosoftByteDanceMobile operating systems and app distributionS68
  6. March 2024Licence change

    Redis moves from BSD to RSALv2 and SSPL

    The most-used cache in software stopped being open source in order to charge cloud providers, and lost the community's goodwill within days.

    RedisAmazonGoogleDocument, key-value and vector-capable NoSQLS56
  7. March 2024Spin-off

    Valkey forks Redis under the Linux Foundation

    A foundation-backed fork appeared within days, backed by the very cloud providers the licence change targeted, and became the default in major managed services.

    RedisAmazonGoogleOracleDocument, key-value and vector-capable NoSQLS56
  8. December 2025Licence change

    The Model Context Protocol moves to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation

    Neutral governance for the connector layer that agents use to reach tools and data. If agents choose tools, being callable becomes a distribution channel, the way being findable became one for search.

    AnthropicOpenAIGoogleMicrosoftAmazonCloudflareAI and model infrastructureData integration and platformsS49
  9. July 2026Milestone

    Cloud infrastructure grows 43% in a quarter, its fastest in eight years

    Twenty years in, the largest infrastructure market in software re-accelerated because AI training and inference demand arrived on top of a base that was already growing.

    AmazonMicrosoftGoogleOracleCoreWeaveCloud infrastructure servicesGPU and AI capacity cloudS03, S03b
  10. July 2026Milestone

    AWS holds 28% of cloud infrastructure, down from 34% in 2017

    The leader lost six points of share while the top three gained: the market became more oligopolistic and less dominated at the same time, which is why the Atlas plots HHI and top-3 share together.

    AmazonMicrosoftGoogleCloud infrastructure servicesS03, S04
