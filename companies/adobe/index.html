Seven axes, each scored 0–5. These are Modeled judgments made in this project against the published rubric, not measurements. The rationale below says why.

  • Network 3PSD and PDF are the interchange formats between agencies, clients and printers, so each adopter pulls in collaborators.

  • Scale 3one creative R&D base amortises across a subscription estate a challenger cannot fund.

Events (3)

  1. 1982Launch

    Adobe is founded and builds PostScript into a printing standard

    Owning a format rather than an application gave Adobe a moat that survived every platform shift for four decades, later repeated with PDF.

    AdobeDesign and creative softwareB01
  2. May 2013Pricing shift

    Adobe ends perpetual Creative Suite licences in favour of Creative Cloud

    Accepted a year of depressed reported revenue and a customer revolt to move the whole base to subscription; by fiscal 2025 subscription was 96% of revenue.

    AdobeDesign and creative softwareS66, S14
  3. December 2023Acquisitionabandoned

    Adobe abandons its acquisition of Figma

    Abandoned under UK and EU scrutiny, with Adobe's Form 8-K recording a cash payment to Figma 'in the previously agreed amount of one billion dollars ($1,000,000,000)': one of the few times regulators stopped a software roll-up outright rather than shaping it.
