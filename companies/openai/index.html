  1. November 2022Platform shift

    OpenAI releases ChatGPT as a research preview

    Made natural language a universal software interface and started the fastest consumer adoption curve ever recorded, redirecting a decade of capital within eighteen months.

  2. 2025Milestone

    AI takes close to half of global venture funding

    Capital is the earliest leading indicator of a new market: AI's share of global venture funding rose from about a third in 2024 to close to half in 2025, with foundation-model companies raising roughly $80B of it.

  3. December 2025Licence change

    The Model Context Protocol moves to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation

    Neutral governance for the connector layer that agents use to reach tools and data. If agents choose tools, being callable becomes a distribution channel, the way being findable became one for search.

    AnthropicOpenAIGoogleMicrosoftAmazonCloudflareAI and model infrastructureData integration and platformsS49
  4. 2026Pricing shift

    Inference prices keep falling by roughly an order of magnitude a year

    Epoch AI tracks declines of roughly 9x to 900x per year depending on the task at a fixed capability level, which is what makes per-task AI economic in categories that could not afford it a year earlier.
