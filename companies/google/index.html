Moats

Seven axes, each scored 0–5. These are Modeled judgments made in this project against the published rubric, not measurements. The rationale below says why.

  • Network 5search advertising is a two-sided market where a subscale rival is worse for both advertisers and users.
  • Switching 4Workspace identity, ad-account history and cloud architecture each make leaving a funded programme.
  • Scale 5global data centres, custom silicon and index infrastructure sit at a scale only a few firms reach.
  • Data 5the query-and-click loop is the product, and it is the reason the leader stays ahead.
  • Brand 5'google it' is the category verb.
  • Ecosystem 5Android and Chrome development are taught skills and career paths.
  • Regulatory 4DMA gatekeeper obligations and antitrust remedies now define what the company may bundle, which cuts against it as much as for it.
Network effects5 / 5
A market-wide two-sided network makes the leading position self-reinforcing and a subscale rival structurally worse for its users.
Switching costs4 / 5
Deep customisation, bespoke integrations and certified administrators make replacement a multi-year programme.
Scale economies5 / 5
Minimum efficient scale is so high that only a handful of firms worldwide can operate at competitive cost.
Data advantage5 / 5
The feedback loop is the product: quality gains from data are the main reason customers choose the leader over a technically similar rival.
Brand5 / 5
The brand is the category noun, and buyers describe the job using the vendor's name.
Ecosystem5 / 5
The ecosystem is a labour market and a curriculum: skills are taught, hired for and career-defining, which locks in the next generation of buyers.
Regulatory4 / 5
The vendor is named in or certified under regulation, so buyers in the sector face additional risk using an alternative.

    The European Commission designates six gatekeepers under the Digital Markets Act

  5. March 2024Licence change

    Redis moves from BSD to RSALv2 and SSPL

    The most-used cache in software stopped being open source in order to charge cloud providers, and lost the community's goodwill within days.

    Valkey forks Redis under the Linux Foundation

    The Model Context Protocol moves to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation

    Neutral governance for the connector layer that agents use to reach tools and data. If agents choose tools, being callable becomes a distribution channel, the way being findable became one for search.
