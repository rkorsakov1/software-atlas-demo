Moats

Seven axes, each scored 0–5. These are Modeled judgments made in this project against the published rubric, not measurements. The rationale below says why.

  • Network 5Office file formats and Teams presence make a subscale alternative worse for everyone a customer works with, which is the oldest network effect in enterprise software.
  • Switching 5Active Directory, Office and Azure sit under revenue-critical and regulated processes, and replacement is treated as existential by IT leadership.
  • Scale 5global data centres plus one R&D base spread across every enterprise workload is a scale only a few firms reach.
  • Data 4telemetry across operating systems, code and productivity work is exclusive and compounds with deployment.
  • Brand 5the default nobody has to justify, in every category it enters.
  • Ecosystem 5the Microsoft partner channel and certification ladder are a labour market and a curriculum.
  • Regulatory 3antitrust history and EU commitments on Teams now constrain the bundling that built the position.
Network effects5 / 5
A market-wide two-sided network makes the leading position self-reinforcing and a subscale rival structurally worse for its users.
Switching costs5 / 5
The product is embedded in regulated or revenue-critical processes, and replacement risk is treated as existential by the buyer.
Scale economies5 / 5
Minimum efficient scale is so high that only a handful of firms worldwide can operate at competitive cost.
Data advantage4 / 5
The data asset is both hard to assemble and legally or practically exclusive, and it compounds with deployment.
Brand5 / 5
The brand is the category noun, and buyers describe the job using the vendor's name.
Ecosystem5 / 5
The ecosystem is a labour market and a curriculum: skills are taught, hired for and career-defining, which locks in the next generation of buyers.
Regulatory3 / 5
Accreditation, audit history or regulator-facing filings create a multi-year barrier for new entrants.

Events (30)

  1. 1975Launch

    Microsoft is founded to sell language tools for microcomputers

    Began as a developer-tools company, a starting position it returned to fifty years later through GitHub and coding agents.

    MicrosoftDeveloper toolsB01
  2. August 1981Platform shift

    The IBM PC ships with an open architecture and a licensed MS-DOS

    IBM kept the brand and lost the platform: because Microsoft could license DOS to clone makers, the operating system rather than the machine became the control point.

    IBMMicrosoftOperating systemsB01
  3. 1985Launch

    Microsoft ships the first version of Windows

    Unremarkable on release, but it started the graphical transition that competitors were late to and that decided the application wars of the early 1990s.

    MicrosoftOperating systemsB01
  4. 1990Bundling

    Microsoft bundles Word, Excel and PowerPoint into Microsoft Office

    The canonical software bundle: priced below the sum of the best-of-breed leaders and sold through the OEM channel Microsoft already controlled. Within five years neither standalone leader was independent.

    MicrosoftLotus DevelopmentWordPerfectProductivity suitesB01
  5. August 1995Platform shift

    Windows 95 ships and consolidates the desktop platform

    Settled the desktop question for a decade and gave Microsoft a distribution channel into which it could place any adjacent product at a price of zero.

    MicrosoftOperating systemsB01
  6. 1996Bundling

    Microsoft bundles Internet Explorer with Windows at no additional charge

    Platform envelopment in its purest form: a product with a price competing against the same product at zero, distributed by the operating system every PC shipped with.

    MicrosoftNetscapeOperating systemsB06, B07
  7. May 1998Regulation

    United States v. Microsoft is filed

    The case that defined the limits of bundling by a dominant platform and constrained Microsoft's conduct through the years when Google and Firefox were establishing themselves.

    MicrosoftNetscapeOperating systemsB06
  8. April 2000Regulation

    District court finds Microsoft liable for monopoly maintenance and orders a breakup

    The only time a US court ordered the breakup of a major software company; the remedy was reversed on appeal a year later.

    MicrosoftOperating systemsB06
  9. June 2001Regulation

    The D.C. Circuit upholds monopoly-maintenance liability but vacates the breakup

    Established the modern US standard for platform tying and set the expectation that remedies would be behavioural rather than structural.

    MicrosoftOperating systemsB06
  10. November 2002Regulation

    The US v. Microsoft settlement imposes conduct remedies

    Interoperability disclosure and OEM freedom rather than divestiture. Whether that was sufficient is the question every subsequent platform case has re-litigated.

    MicrosoftOperating systemsB06
  11. February 2010Launch

    Windows Azure reaches general availability

    Four years behind AWS, but attached to every enterprise agreement and identity directory Microsoft already owned, which is what let it close the gap.
