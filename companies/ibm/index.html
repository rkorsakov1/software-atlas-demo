Events (9)

  1. January 1969Regulation

    US Department of Justice files its antitrust suit against IBM

    Dropped in 1982 without a judgment, but the threat shaped IBM's conduct for thirteen years and is widely credited as a proximate cause of the 1969 unbundling decision.

    IBMOperating systemsB01
  2. June 1969Unbundling

    IBM announces it will price software and services separately from hardware

    Created the commercial software products industry: once the dominant platform charged for software, independent vendors could compete against a priced product rather than a free one. Every later unbundling in this dataset repeats the mechanism.

    IBMOperating systemsDatabasesB03, B01
  3. 1970Launch

    Codd publishes the relational model of data

    Gave the database market a formal foundation and a portable query language, which let database software detach from any one hardware vendor.

    IBMDatabasesB01
  4. August 1981Platform shift

    The IBM PC ships with an open architecture and a licensed MS-DOS

    IBM kept the brand and lost the platform: because Microsoft could license DOS to clone makers, the operating system rather than the machine became the control point.

    IBMMicrosoftOperating systemsB01
  5. 1995Acquisitioncompleted

    IBM acquires Lotus

    IBM bought the loser of the suite war for its groupware franchise, Notes, which became a large installed base but never recovered the desktop.

    IBMLotus DevelopmentProductivity suitesCollaboration and work managementB01
  6. 2001Acquisitioncompleted

    IBM acquires the Informix database business

    Consolidated the second tier of relational databases into the incumbents just as open-source engines began taking the low end.

    IBMInformixDatabasesB01
  7. July 2019Acquisitioncompleted

    IBM closes its acquisition of Red Hat

    $34BReported

    The largest software acquisition of its era and the clearest valuation ever placed on a commercial open-source business: IBM bought the hybrid-cloud position it could not build.

    IBMRed HatServer operating systems and enterprise LinuxVirtualization and private cloudS40
  8. November 2021Spin-off

    IBM separates its managed-infrastructure business as Kyndryl

    IBM cut loose the labour-intensive half of its services business to reweight the company towards hybrid cloud and software. It also breaks IBM's revenue series: FY2020 was filed at $73.620B on the pre-spin basis, FY2021 at $57.350B on the post-spin basis, and IBM's own pro forma restated FY2020 to $57.870B on the later basis — so the apparent 22% fall is a divestiture, not a collapse in demand.

    IBMIT service managementCloud infrastructure servicesS80, S81
  9. February 2025Acquisitioncompleted

    IBM closes its acquisition of HashiCorp

    $6.4BEstimated

    The second commercial open-source infrastructure company absorbed by IBM, and a reminder that a restrictive licence protects revenue better than it protects independence.

    IBMHashiCorpVirtualization and private cloudDeveloper toolsS47
