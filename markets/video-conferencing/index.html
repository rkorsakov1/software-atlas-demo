Video meetings

Horizontal appsMatureBand S

A market that expanded ten-fold in a year and then had to prove it was a product rather than a feature. Its answer, like Slack's, was to build adjacent products before the bundle caught up.

What is counted

Hosted real-time video meeting services sold per host or per room, including webinar and contact-centre extensions built on the same infrastructure.

Modeled order-of-magnitude band S ($5-20B): start from the combined revenue of the leading vendors in the category, divide by an assumed combined share, and round out to a band, sanity-checked against the cited worldwide spending total. Basis: Zoom revenue plus the addressable share of bundled meeting services. The midpoint is a layout device; only the low-high span is a claim.
