Mobile operating systems and app distribution

InfrastructureMatureBand L

A duopoly whose take rate has become a regulated variable rather than a commercial one. Every change in the commission is now negotiated with courts and regulators as much as with developers.

What is counted

Mobile platform economics: store commissions on paid apps and in-app purchases, plus licensing and services tied to the mobile OS. Hardware revenue is excluded.

Band L ($60B–$150B). Modeled order-of-magnitude band L ($60-150B): start from the combined revenue of the leading vendors in the category, divide by an assumed combined share, and round out to a band, sanity-checked against the cited worldwide spending total. Basis: disclosed app-store billings and platform services revenue of the two dominant stores. The midpoint is a layout device; only the low-high span is a claim.

Pricing model
Platform commission of 15-30% on store transactions, with regulated alternatives emerging in several jurisdictions

Buyer: App developers as suppliers; consumers as end payers

Events (6)

  1. June 2007Platform shift

    The iPhone ships and starts the mobile platform era

    The counter-example to disruption theory: an expensive product from an outsider entered at the high end and won, because the platform, not the price, was the innovation.

    AppleMobile operating systems and app distributionS61, B05
  2. July 2008Launch

    Apple opens the App Store with a platform commission on every sale

    Made the platform owner the distributor, the payment processor and the rule-setter at once. The commission it established became the most litigated number in software.

    AppleMobile operating systems and app distributionS61
  3. September 2021Regulation

    Epic v. Apple produces an anti-steering injunction

    Left the platform commission intact but required Apple to let developers point users to other ways to pay, the first crack in app-store economics in the United States.

    AppleEpic GamesMobile operating systems and app distributionS53
  4. September 2023Regulation

    The European Commission designates six gatekeepers under the Digital Markets Act

    Moved platform regulation from case-by-case litigation to ex-ante obligations, the biggest structural change in software competition law since US v. Microsoft.

    GoogleAmazonAppleByteDanceMetaMicrosoftMobile operating systems and app distributionSocial and messagingMarketing and advertising technologyS67
  5. March 2024Unbundling

    Digital Markets Act obligations take effect for designated gatekeepers

    Alternative app stores, third-party payment options and choice screens arrived in the EU, creating the first large natural experiment in whether mandated unbundling shifts share.

    AppleGoogleMetaAmazonMicrosoftByteDanceMobile operating systems and app distributionS68
  6. April 2025Regulation

    Apple is found to have wilfully violated the anti-steering injunction

    The court barred any commission on linked-out purchases, producing the first real direct-to-consumer web payments for iOS apps in the United States; affirmed on appeal in December 2025.

    AppleEpic GamesMobile operating systems and app distributionS53
