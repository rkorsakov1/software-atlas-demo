Virtualization and private cloud

InfrastructureDecliningBand S

A mature, highly concentrated market being harvested rather than grown: post-acquisition repricing raised revenue per customer while pushing a visible minority of customers toward alternatives.

What is counted

Hypervisors, private-cloud stacks and hyperconverged infrastructure software licensed per socket, core or node for customer-operated data centres.

Infrastructure
Maturity

Band S ($5B–$20B). Modeled order-of-magnitude band S ($5-20B): start from the combined revenue of the leading vendors in the category, divide by an assumed combined share, and round out to a band, sanity-checked against the cited worldwide spending total. Basis: VMware subscription revenue under Broadcom plus Nutanix and OpenShift-class platforms. The midpoint is a layout device; only the low-high span is a claim.

Pricing model
Per-core subscription bundles after the industry's move away from perpetual per-socket licences

Buyer: Infrastructure and IT operations leadership
