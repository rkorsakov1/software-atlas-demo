Collaboration and work management

Horizontal appsMatureBand M

The market where bundling and best-of-breed fought most visibly, and where the bundle won on distribution before competition law arrived eight years later to test whether unbundling can restore a lost position.

What is counted

Team chat, video meetings, shared documents and knowledge bases, and work or project management, sold per seat and usually adopted bottom-up before a central contract.

Category
Horizontal apps
Maturity
Mature
Origin
2003

The year the Atlas dates the market's formation, not any one vendor's founding.

Annual spend
$40B ($20B–$60B)2025Modeled

Band M ($20B–$60B). Modeled order-of-magnitude band M ($20-60B): start from the combined revenue of the leading vendors in the category, divide by an assumed combined share, and round out to a band, sanity-checked against the cited worldwide spending total. Basis: Zoom, Atlassian work-management, Asana, monday.com, Notion and the addressable portion of bundled suite revenue. The midpoint is a layout device; only the low-high span is a claim.

Pricing model
Freemium then per seat per month, with heavy discounting when bundled into a productivity suite

Buyer: Team leads and department heads, ratified later by IT

Events (6)

  1. 1995Acquisitioncompleted

    IBM acquires Lotus

    IBM bought the loser of the suite war for its groupware franchise, Notes, which became a large installed base but never recovered the desktop.

    IBMLotus DevelopmentProductivity suitesCollaboration and work managementB01
  2. March 2017Bundling

    Microsoft Teams rolls out to Office 365 customers worldwide

    The Office playbook applied to team messaging: include the challenger's category in a suite the customer already pays for, and let procurement do the rest.

    MicrosoftSlackCollaboration and work managementTeam messagingProductivity suitesS65
  3. July 2020Regulation

    Slack files an EU competition complaint over the bundling of Teams

    Started the case that produced binding unbundling commitments five years later, by which time the complainant had been acquired.

    SlackMicrosoftTeam messagingCollaboration and work managementS26
  4. July 2021Acquisitioncompleted

    Salesforce completes its acquisition of Slack

    $27.7B ($27.1B–$27.7B)Reported

    Best-of-breed did not beat bundled distribution; it sold to a rival suite. The outcome is the strongest single data point for the claim that distribution beats product in horizontal software.

    SalesforceSlackMicrosoftTeam messagingCollaboration and work managementCRMS82, S44
  5. September 2025Unbundling

    The European Commission accepts binding commitments to unbundle Teams

    Microsoft must sell suites without Teams at a lower price, let long-term licensees switch, and provide interoperability and data portability for up to ten years. The Atlas treats it as a live test of whether unbundling restores competition eight years after the tie.

    MicrosoftSlackSalesforceCollaboration and work managementTeam messagingProductivity suitesS26
  6. August 2026Milestone

    Atlassian reports 26% growth, a data point against AI shrinking seat-based SaaS

    FY2026 revenue of $6.57B with a 20% free-cash-flow margin gives a Rule of 40 score of 46, in the category closest to the coding agents that are supposed to be displacing seats.
