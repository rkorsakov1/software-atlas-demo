CRM

Horizontal appsMatureBand L

The market where SaaS was proved, and now the market where the seat itself is being questioned. If an agent resolves a case without a licensed human, the unit of pricing has to change, and CRM is where that experiment is running at scale.

What is counted

Systems of record for customer relationships: sales force automation, customer service, field service and marketing automation, priced per seat and increasingly per agent action.

Events (6)

  1. 1996Launch

    Siebel Systems goes public as the leader in licensed CRM

    Defined enterprise CRM as a seven-figure licence with a long implementation, the exact profit model a subscription entrant could counter-position against.

    Siebel SystemsCRMB01
  2. 1999Launch

    Salesforce is founded to deliver CRM as a multi-tenant subscription

    Counter-positioning: the incumbent could not copy subscription pricing without destroying its own licence revenue, so it did not, until it was too late.

    SalesforceSiebel SystemsCRMB01
  3. January 2006Acquisitioncompleted

    Oracle completes its acquisition of Siebel Systems

    The licensed CRM leader was absorbed rather than defeated in the market, leaving the subscription challenger to define the category on its own terms.

    OracleSiebel SystemsCRMS52
  4. July 2021Acquisitioncompleted

    Salesforce completes its acquisition of Slack

    $27.7B ($27.1B–$27.7B)Reported

    Best-of-breed did not beat bundled distribution; it sold to a rival suite. The outcome is the strongest single data point for the claim that distribution beats product in horizontal software.

    SalesforceSlackMicrosoftTeam messagingCollaboration and work managementCRMS82, S44
  5. November 2025Acquisitioncompleted

    Salesforce completes its acquisition of Informatica

    $8B ($7.5B–$8.5B)Reported

    The suite pitch changed from 'one CRM' to 'one governed data layer for agents': Salesforce bought catalog, governance and master data rather than another application.

    SalesforceInformaticaData integration and platformsCRMS84, S74, S12
  6. February 2026Pricing shift

    Salesforce reports Agentforce ARR of $800M and repricing toward agent outcomes

    Agentforce ARR grew 169% year on year with Agentforce and Data 360 together at $2.9B, and more than 60% of bookings came from existing customers expanding, which is evidence for 'agents add' rather than 'agents replace'.
