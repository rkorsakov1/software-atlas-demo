AI and model infrastructure

InfrastructureScalingBand M

The layer with the fastest price declines and the fastest revenue growth in the dataset at the same time. Both are true because demand for frontier capability grows faster than the price of last year's capability falls.

What is counted

Foundation model access and the tooling around it: hosted model APIs, fine-tuning, inference serving, orchestration frameworks, evaluation and vector retrieval. This is Gartner's narrow 'AI models' line, not its much wider 'AI software' line of $452B for 2026, which overlaps the whole software forecast and is therefore never used to size a market here.

Events (5)

  1. 2025Milestone

    AI takes close to half of global venture funding

    Capital is the earliest leading indicator of a new market: AI's share of global venture funding rose from about a third in 2024 to close to half in 2025, with foundation-model companies raising roughly $80B of it.

    OpenAIAnthropicAnysphere (Cursor)AI and model infrastructureFoundation model APIsS23, S22
  2. December 2025Licence change

    The Model Context Protocol moves to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation

    Neutral governance for the connector layer that agents use to reach tools and data. If agents choose tools, being callable becomes a distribution channel, the way being findable became one for search.

    AnthropicOpenAIGoogleMicrosoftAmazonCloudflareAI and model infrastructureData integration and platformsS49
  3. 2026Pricing shift

    Inference prices keep falling by roughly an order of magnitude a year

    Epoch AI tracks declines of roughly 9x to 900x per year depending on the task at a fixed capability level, which is what makes per-task AI economic in categories that could not afford it a year earlier.

    OpenAIAnthropicGoogleDeepSeekFoundation model APIsAI and model infrastructureS24, B10
  4. May 2026Milestone

    Gartner raises 2026 AI spending to $2.59 trillion

    The May update raised the January forecast of $2.52 trillion to $2.59 trillion, a 47% year-on-year rise, with AI infrastructure taking more than 45% of the total. Gartner widened the scope of the category between the two releases, so the revision is not a like-for-like increase, and the AI lines overlap with the overall software forecast, which is why the Atlas never sums them.

    AI and model infrastructureFoundation model APIsS87, S86, S25, S89
  5. August 2026Milestone

    Model-lab revenue reaches enterprise-software scale

    Anthropic's annualised run rate was reported at $65B at the end of July 2026 against roughly $10B of revenue for all of 2025, with OpenAI reported at $40B. Run rate is the latest month annualised, not GAAP revenue, and the Atlas plots it as estimated.
